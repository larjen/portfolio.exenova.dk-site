Buttons

Primary buttons include links and contact actions; icons can lead their labels, which use the shared button typeface (`type-button`). Secondary uses a border in the primary background tone. Ghost has no border. Icon controls include Home, Arrow, Search and Sort. The Graph/Timeline toggler also shows its disabled timeline state. Every control has a 48px minimum touch target.

Search & dropdowns

The search field and Type dropdown share a pale surface derived from the gray highlight. Dropdowns are popovers with a fine pointer on desktop, bottom sheets otherwise. Type options are tinted when hovered or selected. The pill variant shows only the current option with its colour dot. The graph's grouping reads as a sentence, subject by group: the subject is always the type chip.

Bars & sheets

The reader pane's toolbar stays on one row. When the toolbar is narrow, Search becomes a fixed-width icon and expands when focused, hiding Type and Sort until focus leaves. The pager bar pins below the pane when results span more than one page. The mobile bottom bar switches between Reader and Graph and is shown in place here at every width. Dropdowns still open as bottom sheets on touch screens.
