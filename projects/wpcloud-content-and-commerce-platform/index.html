WPCloud content and commerce platform

Aug 2016 – Sep 2022

The WPCloud platform itself – a content layer tied directly to the Magento store, combining an SEO-optimized e-commerce blog, social media integrations and in-content conversion elements.

WPCloud was the platform Visma Digital Commerce ran for more than 100 Nordic e-commerce clients. It treated content and store as one site: blog and store shared the same URL, an SEO-optimised e-commerce blog brought visitors in, social media integrations sent traffic to the store, and campaigns, lotteries and product carousels inside blog posts let readers act on what they read. Access was split into roles, with separate rights for client users, editors and Visma administrators.
