Executive MBA – Niels Brock

Jan 2021 – Dec 2023

Executive MBA strengthening an experienced technology leader’s foundation in strategy, people development, finance, operations and international marketing.

Completed alongside an established career in technology and management, the Executive MBA at Niels Brock strengthened Lars’s ability to connect technical decisions with commercial priorities, organizational needs and business performance. The programme is also referred to as MBA Go.

The programme comprised six taught modules and a final dissertation:

  • Strategy & Innovation: strengthened strategic thinking through the critical assessment of strategy models, their practical application and the role of innovation in competitive advantage and organizational change.
  • Managing & Developing People: strengthened people leadership and employee development, linking individual capabilities to business goals and examining how organizational values, knowledge and behaviour shape change.
  • Cross Cultural Management & Ethics: developed a stronger framework for considering organizational values, cultural differences, stakeholder consequences and ethical management decisions.
  • Financial & Economic Analysis: strengthened the understanding of how financial management and economic conditions interact, supporting more informed commercial and organizational decisions.
  • Operations & Supply Chain Management: strengthened the understanding of how organizations turn resources into products and services, including planning, control and the relationship between internal operations and supply chains.
  • Global Marketing Management: strengthened market analysis and international marketing strategy, including differentiation, branding and communication across markets and cultures.

The MBA complemented Lars’s technical education and practical management experience with a broader theoretical foundation. Its coursework documents areas of study; professional application and results are described separately in the vault’s projects and accomplishments.
